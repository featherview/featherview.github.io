Look through photos

Featherview is a fast, keyboard-driven image viewer for the Mac. Open one picture, flip through the whole folder, rotate, delete, and batch-convert the keepers.

Features

Everything you need to sort a folder of photos. Nothing in the way.

One window, the image, and the keyboard. Panels and dialogs show up only when you ask for them.

Opens instantly

Double-click a picture in Finder and the rest of the folder is ready behind it, in Finder order.

Keyboard first

Every viewer action is a single key. No modifiers, no menus to dig through.

Lossless rotation

R and L turn JPEG, HEIC and TIFF without re-encoding a single pixel.

Safe by default

Delete goes to the Trash. Rotations are atomic. Batch output never overwrites a file.

Info panel

Camera, lens, exposure, aperture, ISO and date taken, one key away.

Truly native

Mac menus, full screen, “Open With”, drag and drop, Dark Mode and colour management.

Speed

Speed is the feature.

Images are decoded at screen size, off the main thread, while the neighbours load in the background. Measured, not promised: these are medians from a release build.

320 ms

From double-click to a 12-megapixel photo on screen, app not running

9 ms

To show the next image while you hold the arrow key, within one screen refresh

10,000

Files in a folder, first image after 314 ms, and the window never freezes

MacBook Pro (M1 Pro, 2021), 12-megapixel JPEGs. Launch: median of 5. Next image: median of 359 while holding →.

Batch

Convert, resize and rename hundreds at once.

Pick a folder, choose a format and size, name the results. It runs on every core, skips files it can’t read, and never overwrites anything.

Formats

Opens what your Mac can open.

Shown upright and colour-managed, straight from macOS’s own image engine.
