Look through photos
at the speed of a keypress.

Featherview is a fast, keyboard-driven image viewer for the Mac. Open one picture, flip through the whole folder, rotate, delete, and batch-convert the keepers.

Features

Everything you need to sort a folder of photos. Nothing in the way.

One window, the picture edge to edge, and the keyboard. Panels show up only when you ask for them.

Opens instantly

Double-click a picture in Finder and the rest of the folder is ready behind it, in Finder order.

Keyboard first

Every viewer action is a single key. The ⌘ shortcuts and a toolbar are there for everyone else.

Lossless rotation

R and L turn JPEG, HEIC and TIFF without re-encoding a single pixel.

Safe by default

Delete goes to the Trash. Rotations are atomic. Batch output never overwrites a file.

Info panel

Camera, lens, exposure, aperture, ISO and date taken, one key away.

Made for the Mac

Mac menus, full screen, Open With, drag and drop, colour management, and HDR on HDR screens.

Speed

Speed is the feature.

Images are decoded at screen size, off the main thread, while the neighbours load in the background. These are measured numbers, not targets.

Next image, when it’s already loaded

19 ms

Median time to show the next or previous image; 95 % of them within 25 ms. A little over one frame at 60 Hz.

377 ms

From double-click to a 12-megapixel photo on screen, app not running

107 ms

To show an image that wasn’t loaded ahead, such as after a jump to the end

383 ms

To the first image in a folder of 10,000 files, and the window never freezes

48 MB

Memory while browsing, however big the folder is

Measured on one Mac: MacBook Pro with Apple M1 Pro, macOS 27, 12-megapixel JPEGs. Launch is timed after a normal quit, so no window is restored. Other Macs will differ.

Batch

Convert, resize and rename hundreds at once.

Pick the images, switch on the steps you need, and watch the preview follow your settings. Save them as a preset for next time. It runs on every core but one, skips files it can’t read, keeps EXIF and GPS data, and never overwrites anything.
